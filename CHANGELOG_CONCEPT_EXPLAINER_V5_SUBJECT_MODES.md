# Concept Explainer V5 — Subject-Aware Learning Tracks

## Scope

This update extends **Concept Explainer only**. Existing Story Studio, H3/video generation, storyboard, video editor, TTS, ComfyUI and other flows are unchanged.

## New learning tracks

- **Technology** — first-principles explanation, practical mental model, correct code/example, usage and beginner mistake.
- **JEE** — Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics. Uses intuition, diagrams/formulas, standard notation/SI units, worked reasoning and competitive-exam misconceptions.
- **NEET** — Biology, Physics, Chemistry. Uses structure/function/process explanations, diagrams, formulas/equations, key facts and misconceptions.
- **General Learning** — adapts the same visual lesson engine to general science, mathematics, business, social studies and other topics.

## Exam Focus

JEE and NEET tracks can enable **Exam Focus**. The planner then adds exam-oriented application thinking, common traps and a small checkpoint/question when it can be answered unambiguously from the lesson. It does not replace conceptual teaching with rote memorization.

## Preserved behavior

- 1-Minute Quick Learn and 3-Minute Deep Dive remain unchanged.
- Progressive narration-synchronized reveal remains unchanged.
- Reference-quality 1920x1080 neon slide renderer remains unchanged.
- Settled/tight neon glow remains unchanged.
- Lightweight FFmpeg animation/video assembly remains unchanged.
- Per-scene image/audio regeneration remains unchanged.
- 24-hour Concept Explainer retention remains unchanged.
