Built with Indic-Speak from Bodhan AI / AI4Bharat.

AI Story Studio can use Indic-Speak (https://huggingface.co/bodhan-ai/indic-speak) for speech synthesis when you start the optional
`tts-indicspeak` service. Indic-Speak is released under the Indic Open Model License v1.0 and builds on Llama-3.2-3B (Meta), the SNAC quantizer
and a fine-tuned Vocos decoder; their licences also apply. Audio made with it carries the same credit requirement when you share it with others
(for example in a video description). The model is not bundled with this project and is downloaded from Hugging Face on first use.
Running it as a hosted service for other people or companies requires written permission from Bodhan AI.
