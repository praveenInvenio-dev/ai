# Subject visuals (NEET / JEE / Science / GK / Tech) + "Tech for Kids" section

## 16 new devices (every format, every look)
Physics: forces (free-body diagram), wave (λ / amplitude), circuit (battery, resistor, bulb, switch, meters).
Chemistry: atom (Bohr shells), molecule (ball-and-stick, CPK colours, double bonds), reaction (balanced equation,
automatic subscripts, condition over the arrow), element (periodic tile).
Biology / GK: pins (labelled leader lines on a generated picture - anatomy, plants, places).
Maths: numberline (points + shaded interval), triangle (sides, angle, right-angle mark), fraction bars.
Exams / GK: quiz (options, answer revealed on a chosen sentence), factcard ("Did you know?").
Kids: mascot "Robo" (happy / wow / thinking / wave), speech bubble, celebration badge.

## Subject-aware planning
Every track instruction now adds the visuals that suit the subject (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Maths, General
Knowledge ...). New tracks: General Knowledge (documentary-style, verified facts, a quiz) and Tech for Kids.
General Learning gains Physics / Chemistry / Biology / Health subjects. Model synonyms map onto the new devices.

## Tech for Kids (ages 6-12) - built on children's-media research
- Familiar recurring guide character in every scene (young viewers learn better from a familiar character -
  Disney Research), pseudo-interaction: Robo asks and the narration PAUSES 1.4 s for an answer (Blue's Clues),
  repetition of the key idea three ways (Blue's Clues "mastery"), gentle story + humour (Sesame Street),
  segmenting / signalling / weeding (Mayer; Ibrahim et al. 2012): one idea per scene, point at it, cut the rest.
- Bright rounded "kids" look (Sniglet, OFL), confetti edges, slower friendly voice, easy words, a 2-question quiz
  and a "Great job!" badge at the end. Works in every lesson format.
