# Concept Explainer v2

## Requested refinements

- Added exactly two lesson choices in the UI: **1-Minute Quick Learn** and **3-Minute Deep Dive**.
- 1-minute lessons target 55–65 seconds, approximately 125–155 spoken words, and 9–11 scenes.
- 3-minute lessons target 165–185 seconds, approximately 380–440 spoken words, and 18–22 scenes.
- Upgraded the lesson-planning prompt to a best-in-class instructional-design flow: hook, mental model, concrete analogy, concept, examples, beginner pitfall, recap/checkpoint as appropriate.
- Visual generation is now wide **16:9**, matching the approved black-background neon infographic/technical-whiteboard reference style.
- Individual scene images are explicitly required; no collages, grids, or multi-panel posters.
- Visuals use realistic/stylized everyday objects, neon outlines, glowing diagrams, arrows, meters, boxes and clean negative space.
- Human characters are explicitly excluded.
- Indian/local day-to-day analogies are encouraged where they naturally clarify the topic.
- Final Concept Explainer video output is now 1920x1080.
- Existing story generation, H3/video generation, storyboard, editor, characters and other pipelines were not modified.
