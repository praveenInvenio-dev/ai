# Increment 36 — Expressive Indian-English creator-tutor narration

## Goal
Match the reference's engaging, conversational Hinglish-creator energy while keeping the requested narration language (English remains English; do not insert Hinglish unless selected).

## Changes
- Expanded the lesson-writing prompt to direct an expressive Indian educational creator performance rather than textbook/audiobook/announcement prose.
- Added a natural performance arc: curiosity hook, anticipation, memorable concept reveal, clear technical explanation, encouraging takeaway.
- Added mouth-first writing guidance, sentence linking, varied rhythm, natural contractions, concise emphasis, and avoidance of repetitive filler and generic hype.
- Improved the TTS acting direction for Concept Explainer tutoring to request conversational Indian-English cadence, expressive intonation, reveal beats, key-term emphasis, and warm payoff.
- Existing voice selection, rendering engines, visuals, APIs, and unrelated features are unchanged.

## Important limitations
- Edge neural voices do not provide a full emotion/acting model. Prompt and punctuation improvements cannot make Edge behave like a high-expression voice-cloning model.
- For a genuine match to a specific speaker, configure a voice model and use a voice reference recording you own or have permission to use.
- End-to-end audio quality must be checked by generating a lesson on the deployed stack; this source package was not rendered here.
